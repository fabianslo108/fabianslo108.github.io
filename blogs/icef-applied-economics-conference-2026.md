On October 16, 2026, ICEF hosted the  **8th International Conference on Applied Economics** in Moscow. I helped organize the conference with Shihe Fu of Wuhan University and Maxim Nikitin of ICEF, and presented my research during the program.

The conference brought together researchers from HSE University, the New Economic School, Wuhan University, and Shandong University. Its sessions covered three broad themes: environment, resources and policy; migration, labour and communication; and consumption, behaviour and climate.

## Presenting my research

I presented **“Good Looks and the Academic Job Market.”** The paper introduces the RVE Score, a reproducible, low-cost measure of physical attractiveness based on computer vision, and examines how attractiveness relates to academic employment outcomes.

Our results suggest that the relationship between attractiveness and employment outcomes disappears once recommendation letters are taken into account. At the same time, more attractive candidates receive longer and warmer letters, with the relationship between attractiveness and letter tone particularly pronounced for women. This points to an indirect channel: rather than operating directly, appearance may shape how candidates are represented in their recommendations.

## A format built around discussion

Each presentation was allocated 30 minutes, but questions about methods and data often came up before the talk had ended. That made discussion part of the research process: participants could probe how results were constructed and assess how robust the evidence was. The conversations continued after the academic program, including at the conference dinner.

Organizing the event and presenting gave me a view of the meeting from both sides: helping bring the program together and joining the discussion as a presenter. The full program and conference recap are available [here](https://icef.hse.ru/en/news/1213874187.html).
